

## Blog Website — blogthespot.app Style

### 🏠 Homepage
- **Hero Section**: Bada heading with tagline — "Your gateway to insightful articles, creative stories, and trending topics"
- **Gradient background** (purple/blue tones) jaise blogthespot pe hai
- **Browse by Category**: Horizontal scrollable category chips/buttons (All, Celebrity Drama, Life Hacks, Shocking Stories, Tech, Entertainment, etc.)
- **Latest Stories Grid**: 3-column card grid with blog post cards showing:
  - Thumbnail image (Unsplash images)
  - Category badge
  - Post title
  - Short description
  - Author name & date

### 📝 Blog Post Page
- Full article page with hero image, title, category, author info, date
- Rich formatted blog content (paragraphs, headings, images)
- Related posts section at the bottom

### 🧭 Navigation
- Sticky top navbar with blog name/logo, Home, Categories, Latest links
- **Dark/Light mode toggle** switch in navbar

### 📂 Categories Page
- All categories listed with post count
- Click to filter posts by category

### 📄 Sample Posts (6-8 pre-loaded posts)
- Mix of entertainment, life hacks, interesting facts, and trending topics
- Each with Unsplash cover image, author, date, full article content

### 🎨 Design & Feel
- Clean, modern UI matching blogthespot's aesthetic
- Gradient hero backgrounds
- Smooth hover effects on cards
- Dark mode support
- Fully responsive (mobile, tablet, desktop)

### Technical Approach
- Static data (no backend needed for now) — all posts hardcoded in the app
- Category filtering on homepage
- React Router for navigation between pages
